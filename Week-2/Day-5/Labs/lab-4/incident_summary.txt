Incident summary (telecom monitoring sample)

Logs analyzed: app.log

Root cause (RCA)
1. Broker timeout — Connection to broker-2 timed out; broker overload or network partition.
2. Consumer lag — Lag exceeded threshold; consistent with unhealthy brokers or stalled consumers.

Severity: High — Production messaging degraded; SLA risk for event processing.

Recommended remediation
1. Restart or replace unhealthy broker processes on broker-2 after quorum check.
2. Scale Kafka consumers or partitions for topics showing lag.
3. Verify network paths; tune timeouts only after ruling out broker resource exhaustion.
