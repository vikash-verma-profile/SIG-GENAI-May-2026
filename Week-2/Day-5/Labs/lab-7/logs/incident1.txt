Airline booking pipeline incident — 2026-05-07

The nightly batch sync from the reservation mainframe failed after a schema change
to the segment table (extra column seat_upgrade_code). The Spark job aborted during
the bronze-to-silver transform because the expected CSV layout no longer matched.

Downstream: Silver and gold reservation tables were not refreshed; booking APIs served
stale availability for 45 minutes until operators reran the job with an updated schema.

Root cause: undeployed schema registry update for segment feeds combined with silent
failure in the DLQ consumer masked the first alerts.
